## Posting Strategy

### Week 1: LinkedIn Short Post
- **File:** `02-linkedin-short-post.md`
- **Goal:** Quick engagement, drive traffic to portfolio
- **Best time:** Tuesday or Wednesday, 9-11 AM EST
- **CTA:** Link to live demo and portfolio case study

### Week 3: Twitter Thread
- **File:** `03-twitter-thread.md`
- **Goal:** Reach broader tech community, potential virality
- **Best time:** Weekday, 10 AM - 2 PM EST
- **CTA:** Encourage retweets, link to demo

### Week 4: Dev.to/Medium
- **File:** `04-dev-to-medium.md`
- **Goal:** SEO, evergreen content, technical credibility
- **Best time:** Anytime (evergreen)
- **CTA:** Follow for more content, connect on LinkedIn

### Week 5: LinkedIn Article
- **File:** `01-linkedin-article.md`
- **Goal:** Establish thought leadership, detailed technical discussion
- **Best time:** Tuesday or Thursday, 9-11 AM EST
- **CTA:** Comment with your experiences, share if helpful

### Week 6: Reddit
- **File:** `05-reddit-post.md`
- **Goal:** Community feedback, technical discussion
- **Best subreddits:** r/MachineLearning, r/webdev, r/cscareerquestions
- **Best time:** Weekday morning (8-10 AM EST)
- **CTA:** Engage with comments, answer questions

## Engagement Tips

### LinkedIn
1. **Post during peak hours:** Tuesday-Thursday, 9-11 AM EST
2. **Engage with comments:** Reply within the first hour
3. **Tag relevant people:** Mention professors, mentors (if appropriate)
4. **Use 3-5 hashtags:** Don't overdo it
5. **Pin to profile:** If it performs well

### Twitter
1. **Add visuals:** Screenshots, GIFs, or diagrams in first tweet
2. **Engage quickly:** Reply to comments in first 30 minutes
3. **Retweet with comment:** Share your own thread after 24 hours
4. **Use relevant hashtags:** #MachineLearning #MLOps #100DaysOfCode
5. **Tag relevant accounts:** @FastAPI, @reactjs (if they engage)

### Dev.to/Medium
1. **Optimize for SEO:** Use keywords in title and headings
2. **Add alt text:** For all images
3. **Cross-post:** Post on both Dev.to and Medium
4. **Engage with comments:** Build community
5. **Share on social:** Link to the article from LinkedIn/Twitter

### Reddit
1. **Follow subreddit rules:** Some require specific flair
2. **Be humble:** Reddit dislikes self-promotion
3. **Engage authentically:** Answer questions, take feedback
4. **Don't spam:** Wait at least 2 weeks between posts
5. **Provide value:** Focus on learnings, not self-promotion
